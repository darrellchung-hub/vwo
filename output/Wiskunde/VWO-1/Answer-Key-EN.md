## Part A

### Chapter 1 — 3D Shapes

1. A triangular prism has two triangular ends and three rectangular side faces: 2 + 3 = **5 faces**. The two triangles have 6 vertices altogether. Its edges are 3 + 3 triangle edges and 3 joining edges: **9 edges**.
2. A cube has 6 faces. Each face is a square, so its net contains **6 squares**.
3. The top view shows length and width: **4 by 3 cm**. The front view of the 4-by-2 cm side shows length and height: **4 by 2 cm**.

### Chapter 2 — Calculating with Numbers

1. The least common denominator is 12: 5/6 = 10/12 and 1/4 = 3/12. Subtract the numerators: 10/12 - 3/12 = **7/12**.
2. First calculate without decimal points: 24 x 35 = 840. There were two decimal places altogether, so 840 becomes 8.40 = **8.4**.
3. 15% = 0.15. The discount is 0.15 x €80 = €12. Subtract it from the original price: €80 - €12 = **€68**.

### Chapter 3 — Graphs

1. A coordinate pair is written as (x, y). Put x = 4 first and y = 2 second: **(4, 2)**.
2. | x | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |

   | y | 0 | 3 | 6 | 9 |
   Points: (0, 0), (1, 3), (2, 6), and (3, 9).
3. The sequence is on, off, then it starts again. Its period is **2 steps**.

### Chapter 4 — Lines and Angles

1. Compare each angle with 90° and 180°: 42° < 90°, so acute; 90° is right; 90° < 137° < 180°, so obtuse; 180° is straight.
2. Adjacent angles on a straight line add to 180°. So 180° - 73° = **107°**. Check: 73° + 107° = 180°.
3. Draw a 5 cm line segment. Draw an arc of radius 3 cm from one endpoint and an arc of radius 4 cm from the other. Their intersection is the required distance from both endpoints. Connect the intersection to both endpoints. Choosing the other intersection gives the reflected triangle.

### Chapter 5 — Ratios

1. Each person needs 3/5 = 0.6 litre. For 8 people: 0.6 x 8 = **4.8 litres**.
2. 25% is one quarter. Divide €84 by 4: €84 / 4 = **€21**.
3. There are 5 + 4 = 9 parts. One part is €72 / 9 = €8. The shares are 5 x €8 = **€40** and 4 x €8 = **€32**. Check: €40 + €32 = €72.

## Part B

### Chapter 7 — Negative Numbers

1. Start at -9 and move 4 to the right: -9 + 4 = **-5**. Start at 6 and move 11 to the left: 6 - 11 = **-5**.
2. In (-3) x 5 the signs differ, so the result is negative; 3 x 5 = 15, giving **-15**. In (-24) / (-6) the signs match, so the result is positive; 24 / 6 = **4**.
3. (-5)^2 = (-5) x (-5) = **25**. 2^4 = 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 = **16**.

### Chapter 8 — Formulas and Equations

1. Combine the like a-terms: 4a + 3a = 7a. Keep -2, giving **7a - 2**.
2. Substitute x = 4: 5 x 4 - 2 = 20 - 2 = **18**.
3. 3x + 4 = 19; 3x = 15; x = **5**. Check: 3 x 5 + 4 = 19.

### Chapter 9 — Perimeter, Area, and Volume

1. Perimeter = 2 x (8 + 5) = 2 x 13 = **26 cm**. Area = length x width = 8 x 5 = **40 cm²**.
2. The frame adds 2 cm to each dimension: outside dimensions are 10 by 8 cm. Outside area = 10 x 8 = 80 cm². Photo area = 8 x 6 = 48 cm². Frame area = 80 - 48 = **32 cm²**.
3. Volume = length x width x height = 40 x 25 x 30 = 30,000 cm³. Divide by 1,000 cm³ per litre: 30,000 / 1,000 = **30 litres**.

### Chapter 10 — Calculating with Variables

1. a. 6x - 2x = 4x; keep +5, giving **4x + 5**. b. 3 x 2 = 6 and a x a = a², giving **6a²**.
2. Substitute a = 5: 4 x 5 + 3 = 20 + 3 = **23**.
3. Substitute x = 4. First, 4² = 16; then y = 16 + 2 x 4 = 16 + 8 = **24**.

### Chapter 11 — A Look at Probability

1. There are 2 equally likely outcomes, and 1 is heads. Probability = favourable / total = **1/2**.
2. The four outcomes are heads-heads, heads-tails, tails-heads, and tails-tails. One of the four is two heads, so the probability is **1/4**.
3. Green first: 4 of 6, so 4/6. Then 3 green marbles remain out of 5, so 3/5. Multiply along the two stages: 4/6 x 3/5 = 12/30 = **2/5**.

### Chapter 12 — Plane Figures

1. The angles in a quadrilateral add to 360°. The known angles total 90° + 80° + 110° = 280°. The fourth angle is 360° - 280° = **80°**.
2. Reflect in the y-axis by changing x only: (3, 2) → **(-3, 2)**. Reflect in the x-axis by changing y only: (3, 2) → **(3, -2)**. Reflect in the origin by changing both signs: (3, 2) → **(-3, -2)**.
3. A square matches when folded along two diagonals and two midlines, so it has **4 lines of symmetry**. A non-square rectangle only matches along its two midlines, so it has **2**.
