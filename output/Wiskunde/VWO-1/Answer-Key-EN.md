## Part B

### Chapter 7 — Negative Numbers

1. -9 + 4 = **-5**; 6 - 11 = **-5**.
2. (-3) x 5 = **-15**; (-24) / (-6) = **4**.
3. (-5)^2 = (-5) x (-5) = **25**. 2^4 = 2 x 2 x 2 x 2 = **16**.

### Chapter 8 — Formulas and Equations

1. 4a + 3a - 2 = **7a - 2**.
2. 5 x 4 - 2 = **18**.
3. 3x + 4 = 19; 3x = 15; x = **5**. Check: 3 x 5 + 4 = 19.

### Chapter 9 — Perimeter, Area, and Volume

1. Perimeter = 2 x (8 + 5) = **26 cm**. Area = 8 x 5 = **40 cm²**.
2. Outside dimensions: (8 + 2) by (6 + 2) = 10 by 8 cm, so 80 cm². Photograph: 8 x 6 = 48 cm². Frame: 80 - 48 = **32 cm²**.
3. Volume = 40 x 25 x 30 = **30,000 cm³**. Since 1,000 cm³ = 1 litre, this is **30 litres**.

### Chapter 10 — Calculating with Variables

1. a. 6x - 2x + 5 = **4x + 5**. b. 3a x 2a = 6 x a x a = **6a²**.
2. 4 x 5 + 3 = **23**.
3. y = 4² + 2 x 4 = 16 + 8 = **24**.

### Chapter 11 — A Look at Probability

1. 1 favourable outcome out of 2 equally likely outcomes: **1/2**.
2. Possible outcomes: heads-heads, heads-tails, tails-heads, tails-tails. One of the four is two heads, so the probability is **1/4**.
3. Probability of green first: 4/6. Then 3 green marbles remain out of 5. Probability: 4/6 x 3/5 = 12/30 = **2/5**.

### Chapter 12 — Plane Figures

1. The angle sum of a quadrilateral is 360°. Missing angle = 360° - 90° - 80° - 110° = **80°**.
2. In the y-axis: **(-3, 2)**. In the x-axis: **(3, -2)**. In the origin: **(-3, -2)**.
3. A square has **4** lines of symmetry. A rectangle that is not a square has **2**.
