# Antwoordmodel — Hoofdstuk 5 — Niveau 2

*Answer Key — Chapter 5 — Level 2*

*Antwoorden bij [de vragen](05-Verhoudingen-Level-2.md).*

Answers for [the questions](05-Verhoudingen-Level-2.md).

1. *10% = 25, 5% = 12,5 en 1% = 2,5. Dus 18% = 25 + 12,5 + 3 x 2,5 = 45.*

   10% = 25, 5% = 12.5, and 1% = 2.5. Therefore 18% = 25 + 12.5 + 3 x 2.5 = 45.

2. *12 / 8 = 1,5 keer zoveel. 5 x 1,5 = 7,5 koppen rijst.*

   12 / 8 = 1.5 times as much. 5 x 1.5 = 7.5 cups of rice.

3. *7,2 x 25 000 = 180 000 cm = 1 800 m = 1,8 km.*

   7.2 x 25,000 = 180,000 cm = 1,800 m = 1.8 km.

4. *Er zijn 11 delen; €132 / 11 = €12 per deel. De bedragen zijn €60 en €72.*

   There are 11 parts; €132 / 11 = €12 per part. The shares are €60 and €72.

5. *Er zijn 10 delen; 80 / 10 = 8 leerlingen per deel. De groepen zijn 24 en 56 leerlingen.*

   There are 10 parts; 80 / 10 = 8 students per part. The groups are 24 and 56 students.

6. *20% van €150 is €30. Nieuwe prijs: €150 + €30 = €180.*

   20% of €150 is €30. New price: €150 + €30 = €180.

7. *Deel beide getallen door 6: 12 : 18 = 2 : 3.*

   Divide both numbers by 6: 12 : 18 = 2 : 3.

8. *€9,60 / 4 = €2,40 per kg. 7 x €2,40 = €16,80.*

   €9.60 / 4 = €2.40 per kg. 7 x €2.40 = €16.80.

9. *2,4 km = 240 000 cm. Op de kaart: 240 000 / 50 000 = 4,8 cm.*

   2.4 km = 240,000 cm. On the map: 240,000 / 50,000 = 4.8 cm.

10. *Na 20% korting blijft 80% over. €64 / 0,8 = €80 was de oorspronkelijke prijs.*

   After a 20% discount, 80% remains. €64 / 0.8 = €80 was the original price.
