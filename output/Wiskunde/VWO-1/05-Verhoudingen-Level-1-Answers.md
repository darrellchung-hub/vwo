# Antwoordmodel — Hoofdstuk 5 — Niveau 1

*Answer Key — Chapter 5 — Level 1*

*Antwoorden bij [de vragen](05-Verhoudingen-Level-1.md).*

Answers for [the questions](05-Verhoudingen-Level-1.md).

1. *2 rode kralen worden 8, dus de verhouding is 4 keer zo groot: 3 x 4 = 12 blauwe kralen.*

   The number of red beads is multiplied by 4 to get 8, so 3 x 4 = 12 blue beads.

2. *€12 / 4 = €3 per schrift. 7 x €3 = €21.*

   €12 / 4 = €3 per notebook. 7 x €3 = €21.

3. *8.*

   8.

4. *15.*

   15.

5. *2 x 1 000 = 2 000 cm = 20 m.*

   2 x 1,000 = 2,000 cm = 20 m.

6. *Er zijn 5 delen; €45 / 5 = €9 per deel. De bedragen zijn €18 en €27.*

   There are 5 parts; €45 / 5 = €9 per part. The shares are €18 and €27.

7. *6 / 3 = 2 appels per kind; 5 x 2 = 10 appels.*

   6 / 3 = 2 apples per child; 5 x 2 = 10 apples.

8. *18.*

   18.

9. *4 x 100 000 = 400 000 cm = 4 km.*

   4 x 100,000 = 400,000 cm = 4 km.

10. *Er zijn 8 delen; 32 / 8 = 4 punten per deel. De scores zijn 12 en 20.*

   There are 8 parts; 32 / 8 = 4 points per part. The scores are 12 and 20.
