# Antwoordmodel — Hoofdstuk 11 — Niveau 1

*Answer Key — Chapter 11 — Level 1*

*Antwoorden bij [de vragen](11-Kijk-op-kans-Level-1.md).*

Answers for [the questions](11-Kijk-op-kans-Level-1.md).

1. *1/2.*

   1/2.

2. *3 even uitkomsten van 6: 3/6 = 1/2.*

   3 even outcomes out of 6: 3/6 = 1/2.

3. *3 rode van 5 knikkers: 3/5.*

   3 red marbles out of 5: 3/5.

4. *1 van de 4 vakken: 1/4.*

   1 of the 4 sections: 1/4.

5. *2: kop en munt.*

   2: heads and tails.

6. *3 gele van 8 knikkers: 3/8.*

   3 yellow marbles out of 8: 3/8.

7. *4: kop-kop, kop-munt, munt-kop en munt-munt.*

   4: heads-heads, heads-tails, tails-heads, and tails-tails.

8. *1/10.*

   1/10.

9. *1 blauwe van 5 knikkers: 1/5.*

   1 blue marble out of 5: 1/5.

10. *3 x 2 = 6 combinaties.*

   3 x 2 = 6 combinations.
