# Antwoordmodel — Hoofdstuk 9 — Niveau 2

*Answer Key — Chapter 9 — Level 2*

*Antwoorden bij [de vragen](09-Omtrek-oppervlakte-inhoud-Level-2.md).*

Answers for [the questions](09-Omtrek-oppervlakte-inhoud-Level-2.md).

1. *Buitenmaat: (10 + 4) bij (6 + 4) = 14 bij 10 cm, dus 140 cm². Foto: 10 x 6 = 60 cm². Kader: 140 - 60 = 80 cm².*

   Outside dimensions: (10 + 4) by (6 + 4) = 14 by 10 cm, so 140 cm². Photograph: 10 x 6 = 60 cm². Frame: 140 - 60 = 80 cm².

2. *34 = 2 x (6 + lengte), dus 17 = 6 + lengte en de lengte is 11 cm.*

   34 = 2 x (6 + length), so 17 = 6 + length and the length is 11 cm.

3. *50 x 30 x 40 = 60 000 cm³. Dat is 60 liter.*

   50 x 30 x 40 = 60,000 cm³. That is 60 litres.

4. *0,75 x 1 000 = 750 cm³.*

   0.75 x 1,000 = 750 cm³.

5. *12 x 5 x 6 = 360 cm³.*

   12 x 5 x 6 = 360 cm³.

6. *Buitenmaat: (12 + 4) bij (8 + 4) = 16 bij 12 cm, dus 192 cm². Foto: 12 x 8 = 96 cm². Kader: 192 - 96 = 96 cm².*

   Outside dimensions: (12 + 4) by (8 + 4) = 16 by 12 cm, so 192 cm². Photograph: 12 x 8 = 96 cm². Frame: 192 - 96 = 96 cm².

7. *60 x 20 x 25 = 30 000 cm³ = 30 liter.*

   60 x 20 x 25 = 30,000 cm³ = 30 litres.

8. *7,5 x 7,5 = 56,25 m².*

   7.5 x 7.5 = 56.25 m².

9. *A: 8 x 5 x 3 = 120 cm³. B: 10 x 4 x 3 = 120 cm³. De inhoud is gelijk.*

   A: 8 x 5 x 3 = 120 cm³. B: 10 x 4 x 3 = 120 cm³. The volumes are equal.

10. *Totale inhoud: 12 x 8 x 5 = 480 cm³. Driekwart daarvan is 3/4 x 480 = 360 cm³.*

   Total volume: 12 x 8 x 5 = 480 cm³. Three quarters of that is 3/4 x 480 = 360 cm³.
