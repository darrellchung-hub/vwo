## Präteritum: haben, sein, werden, and weak verbs

**Level:** VWO 3 · **Time:** 15–20 minutes
**Focus:** Forms and word order in the German Präteritum (simple past)

**Name:** ____________________    **Date:** __________

## Quick reminder

Use the Präteritum, not the perfect tense. The verbs **haben**, **sein**, and **werden** have irregular past forms. Weak verbs use **-te** in the first- and third-person singular: *lernen → ich lernte; sie lernte*. Verbs whose stem ends in **-d** or **-t** add an extra **e**: *warten → ich wartete*.
